Method & Compliance
How both 100-point scorecards (Candidate JD Alignment & Employer Requisition Quality) are computed, and the rules that keep them honest.
Candidate Side: The 100-Point JD Alignment Score (ops-req-audit)
#ComponentMaxHow it is computed
1Requirement coverage45Max weight = base (hard skill/tool/method/cert 3, title/scope 2, soft skill 1) × tier (Required 2.0x, Preferred 1.0x). Match: exact or JD-offered alternative 1.0, same-capability synonym 0.5, missing 0.0
2Placement & recency20Recent role (≤5 yrs) 1.0x, older/undated role 0.8x, Skills list or Summary only 0.4x
3Title, tenure & operational scale15Title match 5, net tenure after merging overlapping dates 5, quantified operational scope 5
4Knockout gates10Required degree, license, certification, or clearance verified. A missing gate is reported as not verified, never as an auto-rejection
5Document & parse hygiene10Deductions for non-standard headers, missing/irregular dates, unexpanded obscure acronyms
Employer Side: The 100-Point Requisition Quality Score (ops-req-jd-audit, Rubric rqs-0.2)
#ComponentMaxWhat it checks
1Gate necessity25Degree required with no equivalency path (G1, -10), advanced degree gate (G2, -4), required years above level benchmark (G3, -5/-8 or -3 if level is ambiguous), non-licensure cert required (G4, -3), GPA/prestige screen (G5, -3), employment-gap screen (G6, -6)
2Requirement load20More than 10 / 14 required items (L1, -4/-8), more than 6 / 8 required tools (L2, -3/-6), competing products from the same family all required instead of offered as alternatives (L3, -4 per family), no Preferred section (L4, -2)
3Clarity & tiering20No Required/Preferred split (C1, -8), vague clichés like rockstar/ninja/fast-paced (C2, -1 each), >40-word compound requirement lines (C3, -1 each), missing responsibilities section (C4, -4)
4Title / level / scope15Missing job title (T1, -5), internal level/req codes in title (T2, -3), manager/director title with no team/budget/scope stated (T3, -5)
5Language & transparency20Missing pay range (P1, -8), unexpanded acronyms outside domain allowlists (P2, -2/-4), potentially exclusionary wording (P3, -3 each), missing EEO/accommodation statement on standalone text (P4, -2), extreme length (P5, -2/-3)
Employer Side (Module 2): Explainable Screening Governance (ops-req-screen)
#GuardrailRegulatory ContextDeterministic Implementation
1Never Auto-RejectsHuman-in-the-Loop AI GovernanceEvery candidate defaults to PENDING_HUMAN_REVIEW until a human reviewer records a disposition via --decisions-json.
2Blind-Mode Score ParityIllinois HB 3773 (ZIP proxy) & ADEA (Grad Year)Strips name headers, emails (redacted@redacted.invalid), phones, URLs, 5/9-digit ZIP codes, and education graduation years with 0.0 pt score drift.
3Candidate Factor NoticesColorado SB 24-205 & NYC Local Law 144Emits plain-language per-candidate notices detailing the 5 scoring components, quoted 1.0x/0.5x/0.4x evidence, and a Right-to-Clarify checklist.
4Segregated 4/5ths TablesEEOC Uniform Guidelines (29 CFR § 1607.4D)Computes group selection rates and 80% impact ratios post-scoring from separately stored voluntary self-ID JSON (--demographics-json).

Grounding Rules & Requisition Sources
Zero fabrication. Rewrites never invent metrics, tools, titles, team sizes, pay ranges, or seniority. Missing facts become [INSERT: ...] prompts.
Competing products are never synonyms. Tableau does not earn Power BI credit; NICE does not earn Verint credit. On the employer side, requiring NICE and Verint and Genesys simultaneously triggers rule L3.
Fetched Greenhouse, Lever (US + EU), Ashby public job-board APIs; USAJobs official API; employer career pages with Schema.org/JobPosting data (after a robots.txt check).
Paste-only LinkedIn, Indeed, SimplyHired, ZipRecruiter, Glassdoor, Monster, CareerBuilder, Dice, Built In, Wellfound/AngelList. Their terms restrict automated access, so these URLs are declined before any network request (exit code 2).
